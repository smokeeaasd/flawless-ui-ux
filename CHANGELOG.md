### Changed

- Redefined the product contract and architecture from an instruction-only skill to a local design toolkit with a shared executable core, original contextual judgment, and evidence-based corrections. Runtime capabilities remain specified rather than implemented; the existing skill, invocation, dependencies, and installer are unchanged.

- Made the short final pattern pass explicit before delivery of creation and redesign, with scoped use in refinement and review, source-level copy/icon inspection when rendering is unavailable, and correction of cumulative arrows and repeated promises while preserving identity and useful affordances.

- Simplified the skill from seventeen Markdown documents and 18,551 words to nine documents and 4,553 words, with a 547-word entry point and direct intervention boundaries.
- Consolidated context, surface purpose, and task guidance into focused product-structure sections; retained visual direction, craft, accessibility, calibration, original studies, contextual prevention, and evidence-based review without a mandatory reading chain.
- Made navigation/detail choices, distinct editorial contributions, requested feature scope, and actual-background contrast checks concrete while preserving visual expression and meaningful functional tests.
- Aligned the product contract, architecture, workflow documentation, and evaluation records with the simplified package; performance and visual-quality improvements remain unverified until host comparisons run.

- Routed broad visual refinement to calibration while keeping clear local adjustments direct; connected craft and review without reopening identity or duplicating editorial responsibilities.
- Rebalanced design guidance to preserve character and finish during refinement, allow contextual visual ambition without a named style, and separate copy cleanup from visual simplification. Recorded the steering audit and remaining behavioral validation.
- Clarified deliberate font-family selection for new identities, separating intended typography from fallbacks and the families shown in instructional studies.
- Reduced redundant action arrows in the visual studies and clarified meaningful directional cues; strengthened full-page review of repeated slogans while preserving useful information and brand voice.
- Connected creation and redesign to a concrete design target and post-implementation comparison, with optional visual references and product-specific adaptation instead of a fixed style menu.
- Strengthened positive design guidance for product fit, deliberate typography, adaptation of starter defaults, and principled use of references; added scoped WCAG 2.2 acceptance checks and corresponding manual evaluation evidence.
- Reduced pressure for symbolic concepts and conspicuous originality; clarified that conventional layouts and descriptive copy can be complete outcomes, with targeted prevention guidance instead of exhaustive style checks.
- Integrated prevention into direction and post-implementation review, with scoped use during refinement and read-only critique while preserving product truth, brand choices, and useful substance.
- Routed the shared skill by intervention, surface purpose, and task structure, with conditional loading and support for planning-only and mixed requests.
- Deepened subject references with information/action modeling, structural direction, proportional visual changes, state feedback, and task-specific review evidence.
- Recorded implementation of the fifteen-file procedure package separately from pending behavioral evaluation of the expanded workflows.
- Recorded user-confirmed skill identification in OpenCode and Codex separately from pending systematic behavior evaluation.
