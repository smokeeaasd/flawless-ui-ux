# Copy from facts and the reader's next question

Use when authoring interface copy or when copy edits are authorized. Visual-only tasks preserve existing wording (report copy defects separately).

## Rules

- **Facts before adjectives:** ground every headline, offer, and note in confirmed product facts (who it serves, what it does, concrete constraints, pricing/terms, next action). Never invent customer counts, testimonials, ratings, or integrations.
- **Read in sequence:** review eyebrow → headline → subcopy → cards → CTA → footer together. Give each band a distinct job; eliminate repetitive reassurance (*"work smarter"*, *"built for your everyday"*).
- **Competitor-swap test:** if a headline sounds equally plausible with a competitor's name pasted in, replace generic adjectives with concrete specifics.
- **Operational clarity:** use consistent object nouns (don't cycle randomly between *invoice*, *bill*, and *receipt*), specific action labels (*"Request quote"*, *"Save draft"*), and calm recovery-focused error messages.
- **Preserve deliberate voice:** keep brand personality, legal requirements, and expressive campaign lines where they genuinely differentiate rather than flattening everything into sterile fragments.
