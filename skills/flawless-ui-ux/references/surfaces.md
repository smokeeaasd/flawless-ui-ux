# Surfaces

Read the section matching what the visitor needs to accomplish. Classify the requested surface rather than the company: an application's marketing page and its billing settings have different jobs.

## Persuasion

Help visitors understand the offer, determine whether it fits, and take an informed next action. The opening should establish what the product is and why the visitor should care; a memorable headline still needs enough context to be understood.

Sequence content around real questions: what it does, how it works, relevant tradeoffs, evidence, and the next step. Give the main action a clear label and enough context to judge it. Keep secondary actions available without making every control compete equally.

**Watch for:** a page that could sell any product after changing a few nouns, unsupported claims, decorative product mockups, and an impressive opening followed by repetitive empty sections.

**Improve by:** showing actual output, making comparisons concrete, editing generic copy, and changing section structure to match the content. Useful repetition can reinforce a decision; novelty in every section is not a requirement.

Use [Content: persuasion](tasks/content-interfaces.md#persuasion) when this priority needs a complete content and action path.

## Task execution

Begin with the question the user must answer and the action they most often take. Organize the screen so they can locate the relevant object, understand its state, and act without decoding decoration.

Set density from the workload. Repeated panels, tables, small labels, and familiar controls can improve scanning when their meaning is consistent. Keep units, periods, comparisons, and data states explicit. Treat increases according to their meaning: more revenue and more failed requests do not deserve the same success styling.

**Watch for:** generic KPI tiles that displace the actual work, ambiguous chart scales, controls with no effect, and important actions available only after unnecessary navigation.

**Improve by:** aligning related values, making the default view useful, prioritizing meaningful exceptions, and keeping actions close to their objects. Preserve the product's established terminology and workflows during refinement.

Use [Data interfaces](tasks/data-interfaces.md) for record-oriented work or [Forms and journeys](tasks/forms-and-journeys.md) for input and state changes. Load the secondary guide only for a region that needs it.

## Reading

Support comprehension through titles, section hierarchy, readable measure, navigation, and contextual examples. Distinguish an explanation, a reference, a procedure, and a warning through structure and wording.

Let readers recover their location and continue after following a link. On long pages, useful headings and navigation matter more than ornamental section breaks. Keep code, tables, and figures readable when the surrounding text reflows.

**Watch for:** overly wide paragraphs, excessive centered copy, headings chosen for appearance rather than meaning, and sticky elements obscuring text or anchor targets.

**Improve by:** tightening the information hierarchy, grouping related material, using clear link labels, and giving complex content an appropriate presentation instead of shrinking it to fit.

Use [Content: reading](tasks/content-interfaces.md#reading) to connect document structure, navigation, and rendered reading checks.

## Exploration

Make the work, collection, or experience easy to encounter and understand. Its strongest material should set the scale and visual character. Navigation can recede visually while remaining discoverable and operable.

Use distinctive composition, sequence, imagery, and motion when they express the work. Preserve a way to move between items, understand their context, and reach contact or other requested actions.

**Watch for:** elaborate entry gates, hidden navigation, interaction that depends on hover alone, and effects that obscure the actual work or monopolize scrolling.

**Improve by:** bringing the artifact forward, making controls legible, and offering a clear path through the experience. A striking transition must remain understandable with reduced motion and smaller screens.

Use [Content: exploration](tasks/content-interfaces.md#exploration) for discovery, item detail, and return-path decisions.

## Mixed surfaces

Identify a dominant purpose and handle embedded regions according to their local job. A product page's application preview should use plausible data, coherent labels, and actual interface structure. A dashboard's help panel should read comfortably without turning into a marketing hero.

Share visual roles across these regions, but allow their density, emphasis, and interaction to differ. Do not apply page-level display typography or a promotional call-to-action pattern to every application panel.
