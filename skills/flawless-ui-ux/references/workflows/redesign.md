# Redesign

Use when replacement of an existing visual approach is authorized. Establish the extent of replacement before choosing a direction: a new page composition can still retain a required palette, logo, or interaction model. A request for a local improvement belongs to refinement.

## Establish the incumbent baseline

Inspect the target route or component, its current visual system, content, and relevant states. Read the actual implementation and use current rendered evidence when available. Do not treat an old screenshot as proof of the present screen.

Record a compact preservation boundary: product facts, terminology, routes, data meaning, integrations, functioning user paths, and explicit brand constraints. Identify shared components whose other consumers remain outside scope. Use [Context](../context.md) to resolve gaps that affect the replacement.

Establish a few useful before/after checks from the visitor's task. For a queue, these may be finding a record, understanding its status, and completing the row action. For a product page, they may be understanding the offer, locating material terms, and reaching the real next action. Do not manufacture a rendered baseline when the environment cannot provide one.

## Diagnose and define the replacement

Separate task failures, structural problems, and the requested change in expression. Explain what the redesign should improve. A desire for a different visual character is a valid objective when explicit; it does not justify changing product claims or behavior.

Use the relevant task-guide sections to reconsider information order and interaction where permitted. Develop the replacement with [Direction](../direction.md): establish product fit, information hierarchy, coherent visual roles, and how they continue throughout the target. Preserve required brand decisions while changing the authorized structure or treatment.

Use applicable [AI slop prevention](../anti-slop.md) sections to diagnose generic composition, unsupported proof, or competing visual signals. Explain their consequence and retain legitimate uses; substituting a different fashionable default is not a replacement objective.

Compare alternatives where they resolve a real choice, such as a record-first workspace versus a monitoring overview. State the consequence for the visitor. Reuse current technical conventions and integrations unless changing them is part of the request. Planning-only work ends with the replacement outline, preservation boundary, and proposed verification.

## Replace consistently within scope

Implement the new composition and roles through navigation, details, forms, dense regions, and applicable states. Use the relevant [Craft](../craft.md) and [Interaction](../interaction.md) sections. An expressive opening followed by untouched stock sections does not complete a full-page replacement; an intentional retained brand treatment is not a defect.

Preserve required information and accessible behavior as presentation changes. A narrow layout must retain a useful route to actions and important data. Avoid improving screenshots by omitting records, suppressing meaningful overflow, or removing difficult states. Changes to global selectors or shared components require attention to their other affected uses.

Keep unrelated routes and product rules intact. Visual replacement does not authorize a new backend, revised tax calculation, or silently removed payment method.

## Compare outcomes and resolve regressions

Recheck the preserved task paths, facts, and data meaning. Compare corresponding content, state, and viewport conditions when before/after rendering exists. Distinguish a changed hierarchy from a change in the underlying dataset.

Assess the result against the stated objective and relevant [Surface](../surfaces.md) priorities. Use [Review](../review.md) for evidence, correction order, and reporting. Fix material regressions and inspect again after changes; verify visual coherence across the complete agreed target.

Confirm that visual cleanup preserves useful content, functioning controls, and explicit brand constraints. Compare the affected task path and composition, rather than counting eliminated patterns or asserting machine authorship.

**Completion:** the replacement serves its objective, the target has a coherent treatment, and preserved facts and behavior still hold. Report actual evidence and any missing comparison or interaction coverage. Do not claim the old version was observed when only its source was available.

## Example: repair queue

A workshop receptionist needs to find a customer's bicycle and mark work ready. Select [Data interfaces](../tasks/data-interfaces.md) and task-execution priorities. Preserve identifiers, customers, estimates, due dates, statuses, the required brand accent, search, filters, and ready-for-pickup feedback.

A replacement can reduce the prominence of summary panels and give the queue, status, and row action clearer roles. Verify that summaries still describe the intended record set, filters still match correctly, and marking a filtered item ready updates both its visibility and the appropriate count. Compare the same queue states at equivalent widths when rendering is available.
