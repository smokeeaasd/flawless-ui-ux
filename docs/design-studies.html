Everyday care

Start with the finish. A sealed tabletop and an unfinished timber surface may need different care. Check the maker's instructions before choosing a treatment.

Use a soft cloth to remove loose dust. Keep standing water away from joints and wipe up spills promptly. A mat beneath a hot cup can help protect the finish.

Spills and marks

Identify the surface and the mark before acting. If the finish is unfamiliar, ask a restorer before applying a cleaning product.
