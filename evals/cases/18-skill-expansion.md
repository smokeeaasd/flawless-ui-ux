## Rubric and evidence

Use **Met**, **Failed** or **Unobserved** on each criterion with a concrete observation. Preserve unfavorable cases, blocked runs and missing checks.

- **Function:** primary task and changed transitions work; data meaning and calculations hold; invalid input can recover; no false integration claims.
- **Access:** primary actions remain reachable at desktop/phone widths; labels and focus are usable; relevant selected automated checks are recorded separately from manual/assistive-technology gaps.
- **Scope:** protected facts, wording, colors, composition, records and behavior survive as specified. No unauthorized features or dependencies.
- **Craft:** task-relevant hierarchy, readable roles, coherent grouping, appropriate density and lower-region continuity are visible in matching captures. Record a reasoned observation, not a numerical score or preference disguised as a defect.

Record actual reference reads, implementation files, browser/interaction checks, source scope and runner errors. Open captures for visual judgment. A model's self-report is attributed evidence, not independent verification. Parent checks must be distinguished from checks the model actually performed. Hash artifacts at completion; do not repair unfavorable outputs before evaluating them. Native, screen-reader, long-term maintenance and real-user outcomes are outside this round.
