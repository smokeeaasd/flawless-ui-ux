# 01: Promotional page

Starting project: empty directory. Follow the [run instructions](../README.md) for invocation and isolation. Only submit the task block below.

## Task

```text
Build a responsive one-page website for Spoke, a fictional neighborhood bicycle repair workshop. Its visitors are everyday riders who need to understand the services and find out how to bring in a bicycle.

Confirmed content:
- Puncture repair: £12. Brake adjustment: £20. Full tune-up: £65.
- Open Tuesday–Saturday, 09:00–18:00.
- Address: 14 Market Lane, Northbridge (fictional).
- Walk-ins are welcome. Staff inspect the bicycle, confirm the work and price, and call when it is ready. Same-day completion is not guaranteed.
- This is a static demo. No booking service, payment, or contact endpoint exists.

Use plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript only where useful. Create index.html and local supporting files. There are no brand assets or photographs. Use system fonts and local assets; no external dependencies or network-loaded resources. Choose a visual direction suited to a practical local workshop. Make the next action and repair process clear, including on a narrow screen. Do not invent testimonials, awards, additional business facts, or working integrations.

Complete the page, verify it with the tools available, and briefly explain the direction, checks performed, and remaining limitations.
```

## Evaluator checks

- The three prices, opening hours, location, walk-in policy, and repair process are accurate and easy to find.
- The main action has a useful, honest destination, such as service or visit information. It does not simulate a successful booking or payment.
- The organizing idea and hierarchy fit a local repair workshop. No particular font, color, or layout is required.
- A study or supplied reference, when used, informs a concrete composition suited to these facts. Do not award credit merely for copying the study's appearance; inspect information order, useful actions, lower content, and narrow-screen adaptation. A reference image is not proof of working behavior.
- The page is complete beyond the first screen, readable at desktop and narrow widths, and free of network resource dependencies.
- Claimed checks match their actual evidence.

Reuse this exact task body for the natural-language and baseline runs. Do not give either run the evaluator checks or output from the other run.
